Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 14, 2026

1. Who we are

Ninar Studio is a private command-line tool operated by the owner of the Sleep Sheep YouTube channel ("the operator"). It runs only on the operator's own computer and is used solely to upload videos to YouTube channels owned and managed by the operator. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

2. Use of YouTube API Services

Ninar Studio uses YouTube API Services (YouTube Data API v3). By using Ninar Studio you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Information sent to YouTube is also governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Information we access

With the account holder's permission (OAuth 2.0), Ninar Studio requests a single scope, https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload, which allows it to:

  • upload videos, with their title, description, tags and privacy settings (videos.insert);
  • set the custom thumbnail of a video it uploaded (thumbnails.set).

Ninar Studio does not read channel analytics, comments, subscribers, playlists, watch history or any data belonging to other users.

4. Information we store

Everything is stored only on the operator's computer. There are no servers, databases, cookies or tracking.

  • OAuth tokens issued by Google, kept in a local folder so the tool can upload without asking for permission each time.
  • Upload records: for each video the tool uploaded, its video ID, link, privacy status and upload time, used to avoid uploading the same video twice.

5. How information is used and shared

The information above is used only to upload the operator's own videos to the operator's own channels. It is never sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising. The only transfer of data is the upload itself, sent directly to YouTube.

6. Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time on the Google Account permissions page: https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking access immediately stops Ninar Studio from uploading to that account.

7. Data deletion

Stored tokens and upload records can be deleted at any time by removing the local files, and they are deleted when access is revoked. Upload records contain only data about the operator's own videos. To request deletion or ask a question, open an issue at github.com/edudaguano/ninar-studio/issues; requests are handled within 7 days.

8. Changes

Changes to this policy will be published on this page with a new effective date.

9. Contact

Open an issue at github.com/edudaguano/ninar-studio/issues.
